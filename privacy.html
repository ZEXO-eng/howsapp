1. Overview

HowsApp is a messaging application that provides account authentication, user profiles, privacy controls and communication features. This Privacy Policy explains what information HowsApp accesses, how it is used, where it may be stored, and the choices available to users.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create or use a HowsApp account, we may process your email address, display name, username, profile image, authentication identifiers and account settings.

Google Sign-In information

If you choose “Continue with Google,” HowsApp requests only the basic account information needed to authenticate you and create your HowsApp profile. This may include your Google account name, email address, profile picture and a Google-provided account identifier.

HowsApp does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Contacts, calendars, or other unrelated Google services as part of normal Google Sign-In.

Messaging and service data

When messaging features are used, the service may process message content, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, reactions, delivery/read information and technical data necessary to provide the conversation. HowsApp does not claim that messages are end-to-end encrypted unless that feature is explicitly provided and identified in the application.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate users and maintain account sessions.
  • Create and display HowsApp user profiles.
  • Provide messaging and conversation features.
  • Apply user privacy and notification preferences.
  • Protect accounts, investigate abuse and maintain service reliability.
  • Respond to support or account-related requests.

4. Google user data

Google user data obtained through Google Sign-In is used only for authentication, account identification and profile creation. HowsApp does not sell Google user data and does not use Google user data for advertising. Access is limited to the functionality described in this policy.

5. Storage and service providers

HowsApp uses Supabase for authentication and backend services. Information necessary to operate the application may therefore be processed or stored using Supabase infrastructure. The use of third-party service providers is limited to providing and securing HowsApp functionality.

6. Data sharing

HowsApp does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with infrastructure providers strictly as needed to operate the service, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Account and service data is retained for as long as it is reasonably needed to provide HowsApp, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations. Users may request account or personal-data deletion by contacting YOUR_EMAIL@gmail.com.

8. Security

HowsApp uses authentication controls and backend access policies intended to protect user information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and users should protect access to their devices and account credentials.

9. Children's privacy

HowsApp should only be used in accordance with applicable age and parental-consent requirements in the user's jurisdiction. If you believe information was provided in violation of applicable requirements, contact us so it can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when HowsApp features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

11. Contact

Privacy and account questions may be sent to YOUR_EMAIL@gmail.com.

بيانات تسجيل الدخول باستخدام Google

عند اختيار "المتابعة باستخدام Google"، يطلب HowsApp فقط بيانات الحساب الأساسية اللازمة لتسجيل الدخول وإنشاء ملفك الشخصي، وقد تشمل اسم حساب Google وبريدك الإلكتروني وصورة الحساب ومعرف الحساب الذي توفره Google.

لا يطلب HowsApp الوصول إلى رسائل Gmail أو ملفات Google Drive أو جهات اتصال Google أو التقويم أو خدمات Google الأخرى غير المرتبطة بتسجيل الدخول.

بيانات الرسائل والخدمة

عند استخدام ميزات المراسلة قد تعالج الخدمة محتوى الرسائل ومعرفات المرسل والمستلم والتوقيتات والرياكشنات وحالات التسليم والقراءة والبيانات التقنية اللازمة لتقديم المحادثة. لا يدعي HowsApp أن الرسائل مشفرة من طرف إلى طرف ما لم يتم توفير هذه الميزة وذكرها بوضوح داخل التطبيق.
